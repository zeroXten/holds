      马步 · Mǎ Bù · horse stance

      Welcome to Mabu.fit

      Shaolin training starts with horse stance: holding a position, still and steady, until legs, tendons and patience grow strong. Mabu turns those holds into a daily practice, with a camera coach and a temple to build.

      1. Set up the camera. Prop your phone 2–3 m away at about waist height, then step back until your whole body is in view. The lines on your body go green when your form is good, and the chips say what to fix.
      2. Hold. Every 10 seconds in good form earns a coin. Keep going past the target for bonus time, which earns faster.
      3. Level up. Reach a hold’s target on 3 different days to unlock its next level.
      4. Come back each day. The hold of the day pays 3× on your first go, and streaks bring bonuses and milestone rewards.
      5. Spend your coins on new holds, your temple, your monk, or bets on yourself.

      The camera runs on your phone only: no video ever leaves it. Progress is saved on this device, so export a backup in Settings now and then.
