How it works

  1. Prop your phone up 2–3 m away, about waist height, screen facing you.
  2. Step back until your whole body is in view. The camera checks your stance as you hold it.
  3. You score points for every second in good form. Better form scores more.
  4. Hit the target on 3 different days to unlock the next level.

The camera is only used on your phone. No video leaves the device.
